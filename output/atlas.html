How much is “drainage”?

the tagging elasticity

The same ₹23,132 crore is 1.3% or 46.0% drainage depending on which of three defensible keyword definitions you apply — a 29-fold range. Hand-adjudicating 300 orders shows why: the medium tier has 100% precision and 98% recall, but 80% of what it flags is bundled “roads and drains” work, where the full amount is charged to drainage. The narrow tier is clean but catches only 6.5% of genuine drainage. Any single number here is a statement about the analyst's keyword list as much as about the city.

Alignment with the ruling party buys a ward nothing, and the hazard penalty is unmoved by party controls. Whatever produces the budget gap, it is not partisan targeting.

Mean flood hazard by councillor party (z)

Hazard is nonetheless politically distributed: opposition INC wards are more flood-prone than BJP wards, and the seven independents are the most exposed of all.

Does the result hold up?

seven robustness arms · Conley spatial SEs

Effect on the drainage share per standard deviation of hazard, each arm re-estimated with ward-clustered and Conley spatial standard errors. Under the narrow definition — dedicated stormwater assets only — the effect is significantly positive (+0.445 pp, p = 0.013). Dedicated drainage does track hazard; bundled road-and-drain money does not. The headline budget penalty ranges −8% to −12.8% depending on how much terrain is absorbed into the controls, and is significant in every specification.
